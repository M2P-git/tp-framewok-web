## React + TypeScript developer for the MVP of an online booking platform (Morocco)

**Fixed price** · **Budget: $2,500** · **Intermediate** · **Duration: 1 to 3 months** · Posted by *Mawid (Rabat, Morocco)*

We are **Mawid**, an early-stage startup based in Rabat. Small service businesses in Morocco
(hair salons, barbers, hammams, beauty institutes, physiotherapists, sports coaches) still take
appointments by phone and WhatsApp. The result: missed calls, double bookings, and many
customers who simply don't show up.

We want a web app where:

- **customers** find a business, pick one or more services, choose a free time slot and book in
  less than a minute, **without creating an account**;
- **business owners** see their daily agenda, confirm or cancel bookings, and mark no-shows;
- **each business page uses the business's own colours and logo**: we will sell the same
  product to hundreds of businesses.

**Phase 1 (this contract):** the front-end MVP in **React + TypeScript**, connected to our mock
API (provided, documented). **Phase 2 (later contract):** the real back-end.

**Must have:** responsive (most customers book from their phone), French first (Arabic later),
fast, accessible, clean code that another developer can take over.

**Nice to have:** a dashboard with key figures for owners, customer reviews, an AI assistant
that helps owners write their service descriptions, a small online shop for hair and beauty
products.

**Deliverables:** source code on GitHub, a deployed demo link, a README, a short video demo.

**Milestones (to be discussed in your proposal):** clickable prototype and backlog → booking
flow → owner agenda, tests and deployment.

**Skills:** React, TypeScript, REST APIs, CSS / Tailwind, Git, testing.

**Screening questions:**

1. Describe a similar project you built. What was the hardest part?
2. How would you prevent two customers from booking the same slot?
3. What would you put in the MVP, and what would you leave for later? Why?

## En français, en résumé

Mawid veut une application web de prise de rendez-vous pour les petits commerces de service.
Les **clients** trouvent un établissement, choisissent des prestations et un créneau libre, et
réservent en moins d'une minute, sans créer de compte. Les **gérants** voient leur agenda du jour,
confirment ou annulent, et notent les absences. **Chaque établissement a ses couleurs et son
logo** : le même produit sera vendu à des centaines de commerces. Ce contrat porte sur
l'interface (React, TypeScript), branchée sur une API simulée fournie ; le serveur viendra plus tard.
